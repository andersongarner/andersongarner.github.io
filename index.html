Vortex Generator Geometry & Airfoil Stall Characteristics

Co-authored a wind tunnel + CFD study on vortex generator size and placement, testing 90 configurations on a modular NACA 4410 airfoil.

Found the initial size/position hypothesis disproven, and used a Kriging surrogate model to identify an optimal configuration reaching a modeled L/D of 56.99.

ANSYS Fluent Wind Tunnel Testing

Supersonic Wind Tunnel Flow Characterization (5/26)

Characterized compressible flow in UVA's supersonic blowdown wind tunnel, validating blowdown models within ~11% of experimental timing.

Cross-validated Mach number across three independent methods using pressure taps, nozzle geometry, and shadowgraph shock angles.

Compressible Flow Shadowgraphy

Material Selection for AH-64 Apache Rotor Blades (12/25)

Performed ANSYS Granta analysis to select rotor blade materials against yield strength, stiffness, fatigue, and cost constraints.

Selected PEEK/IM carbon fiber composite as the optimal material via four derived merit indices.
